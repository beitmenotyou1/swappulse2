# Bookmark Boards

Organise saved content into collections

## What are Bookmark Boards?

Bookmark Boards let you save and organise posts, cards, trade listings, and journals into themed collections. Think of them as Pinterest-style boards for Pokémon TCG content. Boards are mirrored to the AT Protocol and can be public or private.

## What you can do

* **Create boards:** Make themed boards like "Charizard Collection", "Trade Targets", or "Grail Cards".
* **Save content:** Use the bookmark button on any post, card, or trade listing to save it to a board.
* **Share boards:** Public boards are visible on your profile and can be featured in starter packs.
* **Reorder:** Drag and drop items within a board to prioritise your favourites.

## Featuring in starter packs

You can feature a bookmark board in a starter pack to help newcomers discover curated content. The board appears on the pack detail page for anyone viewing the pack.
