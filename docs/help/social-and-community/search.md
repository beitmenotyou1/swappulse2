# Search

Find anything on SwapPulse

## What can I search for?

The Search page lets you find cards, collectors, posts, trade listings, and journals across SwapPulse in one place. Search is federated, meaning it also includes content from connected AT Protocol instances.

## Search types

* **Cards:** Search by card name, set, or TCGDex ID.
* **Collectors:** Find collectors by handle, display name, or DID.
* **Posts:** Full-text search across posts in the firehose.
* **Trade listings:** Find cards offered or wanted in active trades.
* **Journals:** Search long-form journal entries.

## Hashtag search

You can also search by hashtag. Type a hashtag in the search bar or click any hashtag on a post to see all posts with that tag. Follow hashtags to surface relevant content in your For You feed.

## Saved searches

Save frequent searches for quick re-access. Saved searches appear in your sidebar and can be scoped to specific content types.
