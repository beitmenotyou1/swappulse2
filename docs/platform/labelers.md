# Community Labelers

Curated content labels and moderation

## What are Community Labelers?

Community Labelers are accounts that apply labels to content across the AT Protocol. Labels can flag content as sensitive, categorise it by topic, or signal trustworthiness. SwapPulse subscribes to labelers and applies their labels to posts, profiles, and media you see in your feed.

## What you can do

* **Browse labelers:** See all available community labelers on the Labelers page.
* **Subscribe:** Subscribe to labelers whose labels you want applied to your feed.
* **Label content:** If you run a labeler, apply labels to posts and profiles from the label button.
* **Filter:** Labels from subscribed labelers automatically filter or warn about content in your feed.

## How labels work

Labels are AT Protocol records published by labeler accounts. When you subscribe to a labeler, SwapPulse fetches their labels and uses them to filter, warn, or categorise content. Labels are transparent, you can see which labeler applied a label and why.

## Running a labeler

Any collector can run a community labeler. Set up a labeler in Settings, define your labeling policies, and start applying labels. Other collectors can subscribe to your labeler to benefit from your curation.
